EXPLORE THE TOWN

Get to know Oxford, Ohio.

Miami University sits in a small college town. Here are places for you and your family to explore, eat, and stay.

local_activity
THINGS TO DO

Local attractions

park

Miami University Natural Areas

More than 1,000 acres around campus with about 17 miles of hiking trails.

museum

McGuffey House and Museum

A short walk from the Holiday Inn and campus, per hotel listings; check the museum for visiting hours.

palette

Miami University Art Museum

Campus art museum, a good rainy-day stop for families visiting.

theater_comedy

Oxford Community Arts Center

Local arts programming a few minutes from campus.

sports_hockey

Goggin Ice Center

Ice skating and RedHawks hockey, about 1.2 miles from the Hampton Inn.

nature_people

Uptown Parks

High and Main streets, where Uptown events such as MiamiFest take place.

WHERE TO EAT

Uptown restaurants

Uptown food is not covered by Miami meal plans. Descriptions come from Travel Butler County and Miami student media.

bakery_dining

Bagel & Deli

On High Street, less than half a block from campus; known for steamed bagel sandwiches.

local_bar

Mac & Joe's

Established in 1946 and described as Oxford's oldest running bar and saloon.

donut_large

Oxford Doughnut Shoppe

120 S. Locust St.; a Butler County Donut Trail stop making kettle-fried donuts for 50+ years.

restaurant

Skipper's Pub

The first restaurant you see walking Uptown; reported to stay open until 2:30 a.m.

ramen_dining

Phan-Shin Restaurant

104 W High St.; Chinese and Thai, serving the community since 1985.

local_pizza

Paesano's Pasta House

308 S. Campus Ave.; one of dozens of Uptown and nearby dining options.
